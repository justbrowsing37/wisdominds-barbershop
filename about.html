About Us

Style. Confidence. Character.

Wisdominds Barber & Braiders is more than a barbershop — it's a community-driven social enterprise and a proud initiative of Wisdominds Co-operative Canada. We were founded to provide exceptional barbering and braiding services while creating employment, entrepreneurship, and skills development opportunities for aspiring barbers and braiders.

Our mission is to empower individuals through professional training, mentorship, and practical experience, helping them build successful careers and businesses in the beauty and grooming industry.

As we grow, our vision is to expand across Ontario and Canada through a franchise model that creates jobs, supports local entrepreneurs, and strengthens communities through cooperative values.

At Wisdominds Barber & Braiders, we don't just create great styles — we build confidence, careers, and opportunities.

Community Impact

Creating Lasting Impact Beyond the Barbershop

As a social enterprise of Wisdominds Co-operative Canada, we're committed to creating meaningful opportunities that empower individuals, strengthen communities, and promote sustainable economic development.

Youth Empowerment

We inspire and equip young people with practical skills, mentorship, and entrepreneurship opportunities, helping them build confidence, discover their potential, and prepare for successful careers in the beauty and grooming industry.

Skills Development

Through hands-on training, apprenticeships, and professional development programs, we provide individuals with industry-relevant skills that enhance employability and support lifelong career growth.

Employment Creation

Our goal is to create sustainable employment opportunities for barbers, braiders, apprentices, and support staff while fostering pathways to business ownership and franchise opportunities across Canada.

Community Partnerships

We actively collaborate with community organizations, educational institutions, government agencies, and industry partners to expand access to training, employment, and economic opportunities that benefit individuals and families.

The Cooperative Difference

As part of Wisdominds Co-operative Canada, we operate on a cooperative model that promotes shared ownership, collaboration, and community prosperity. We believe that by working together, we can create stronger businesses, empower more entrepreneurs, and build more resilient communities.

Together, we are building careers, creating opportunities, and strengthening communities — one person at a time.
