Barber & Braiding Academy

Learn. Master. Build Your Future.

The Wisdominds Barber & Braiding Academy is designed to develop the next generation of skilled barbers, professional braiders, and beauty entrepreneurs. Our academy combines hands-on practical training with business education to equip students with the technical skills, confidence, and entrepreneurial mindset needed to succeed in today's beauty and grooming industry.

Whether you're beginning a new career, enhancing your existing skills, or aspiring to own your own barbershop or salon, our training programs provide a clear pathway to professional success.

Barber Training

Comprehensive barbering training covering haircutting techniques, fades, beard grooming, shaving, customer service, sanitation practices, and modern barbering trends.

Hair Braiding Training

Professional training in natural hair care, cornrows, knotless braids, box braids, twists, protective styling, and contemporary braiding techniques.

Apprenticeship Program

Gain valuable real-world experience by working alongside experienced professionals in a live barbershop environment while developing practical skills and customer confidence.

Business Coaching

Learn how to build and manage a successful barbering or beauty business through training in entrepreneurship, customer service, marketing, financial management, branding, and business operations.

Certification

Upon successful completion of the program, participants will receive a Wisdominds Barber & Braiding Academy Certificate of Completion, recognizing their training and practical competencies.

Internship Opportunities

Outstanding participants may be offered internship and employment opportunities within Wisdominds Barber & Braiders and future partner locations, providing valuable industry experience and career advancement opportunities.

Roles We're Hiring For

Full-Time Professional Barber

We are seeking experienced barbers who are passionate about delivering exceptional grooming services and outstanding customer experiences.

Responsibilities

  • Haircuts and modern fades
  • Beard trims and line-ups
  • Hot towel shaves
  • Client consultations
  • Maintaining a clean, professional workstation
  • Building strong customer relationships

Requirements

  • Minimum 1 year of professional barbering experience
  • Strong customer service skills
  • Team-oriented attitude
  • Reliable and professional
  • Able to work flexible hours, including weekends

Professional Hair Braider

Join our growing team of talented hair braiders specializing in natural and protective hairstyles.

Responsibilities

  • Box braids & knotless braids
  • Cornrows & stitch braids
  • Twists & faux locs
  • Crochet braids
  • Hair extensions
  • Hair care consultation

Requirements

  • Minimum 1 year of professional experience
  • Strong braiding techniques
  • Creativity and attention to detail
  • Excellent communication skills
  • Passion for helping clients look and feel their best

Barber & Braiding Apprentices

Ready to begin a rewarding career in the beauty industry? Our apprenticeship program provides hands-on training under experienced professionals while you develop practical skills in barbering, braiding, customer service, and salon operations.

Ideal For

  • Recent graduates
  • Individuals changing careers
  • Newcomers with foundational experience
  • Passionate learners seeking professional mentorship
